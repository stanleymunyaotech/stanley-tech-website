04 / AUTOMATION ⤴

Workflow
automation

Making recurring digital work easier to manage by connecting tools, standardising repeatable steps and reducing avoidable manual effort.

Discuss an automation ↗

What can be delivered

  • Review and mapping of a repeatable workflow
  • Form-to-spreadsheet or structured record-capture processes
  • Automated notifications and task hand-offs where supported
  • Routine file, data-preparation or reporting tasks
  • Testing notes, documentation and a practical handover

Typical tools

Who this can help

Project approach

Each workflow is assessed before development. The tools, permissions and integration limits are confirmed during scoping so the result is realistic and maintainable.

HOW WE WORK

A clear process.
No guesswork.

Project scope, timeline and cost are agreed before work begins. The process stays simple so expectations remain realistic on both sides.

01

Conversation

Share the challenge, the people involved, the goals and any existing materials or data. This is where the real need becomes clear.

02

Scope & quote

Agree on deliverables, what is included and excluded, a realistic timeline and a written project quotation.

03

Build & review

Develop the agreed solution. Review key milestones together so adjustments can be made early if needed.

04

Handover

Test the result, share the final deliverables, explain how to use them and outline any recommended next steps.

Pricing

Projects are quotation-based. The fee depends on the agreed scope, complexity, volume of content and any integrations required. No fixed starting price is published because each project needs a defined scope first. After a short conversation you will receive a clear written quote before any work starts.
