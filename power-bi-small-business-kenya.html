Power BI Dashboards for Small Businesses in Kenya

Many small businesses collect sales, customer and inventory information every day but do not always have an easy way to understand what the numbers are saying.

A Power BI dashboard can turn this raw business data into clear visual reports that make it easier to monitor performance and identify important trends.

The goal is not simply to create attractive charts. The goal is to make business information easier to understand and act on.

What can a small business track?

A useful dashboard can bring several important business measurements into one place.

  • Sales and revenue performance
  • Best-selling products
  • Customer purchasing patterns
  • Inventory and stock movement
  • Monthly and daily performance
  • Comparison between different periods

Why use Power BI?

Power BI can help transform spreadsheets and other business data into interactive reports. Instead of manually checking large tables of numbers, business owners can use visual summaries to understand important patterns more quickly.

For example, a sales dashboard could show total revenue, the products generating the most sales and changes in performance over time.

What data can be used?

A dashboard can be built from sources such as Excel spreadsheets, CSV files and structured business records. The exact approach depends on how a business currently stores its information.

The important part is the business question

A dashboard should begin with a business problem rather than with a collection of random charts.

For example, a business might want to know which products sell the most, which months generate the highest revenue, or whether sales are improving over time.

Once the questions are clear, the data and visualizations can be designed around them.
