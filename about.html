THE PERSON BEHIND THE WORK

Combining technical thinking with practical execution.

I'm Stanley Munyao, a technology and data solutions developer based in Nairobi, Kenya. I focus on building professional websites, developing practical software applications and using data to make business information easier to understand and act upon.

My work brings together three closely connected areas: web development, software and systems development, and data analytics. Each serves a different purpose, but they share one objective: creating solutions that address actual needs rather than adding technology for its own sake.

This approach is reflected in projects such as the JAPRES Academy website and portal, an 800-record sales data analytics project, and digital platforms developed around my own technology brand. These projects provide practical experience in translating requirements into working interfaces, structured workflows and useful information.

I am studying Economics and Statistics at the University of Nairobi. This gives me an analytical perspective on data, business questions, quantitative problems and evidence-based decision-making.

My technical work includes HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Python, Microsoft Excel, database-backed application workflows and tools for building and managing digital products. I continue to deepen my capabilities in SQL, Power BI and broader software engineering practices, while keeping project claims grounded in work actually completed.

HOW I APPROACH MY WORK

Understand first. Build with purpose.

Effective technology begins with a clear understanding of the people, processes and objectives behind a project. I approach each engagement with that principle in mind, from the initial requirements to the final handover.

01

Understand the problem

Identify the actual need, understand the intended users, examine existing processes and establish what a successful outcome should achieve.

02

Plan the right solution

Translate requirements into a practical direction, selecting appropriate technologies, defining essential features and keeping the solution aligned with the project's scope.

03

Develop and refine

Build in structured stages, pay attention to usability and functionality, test important workflows and improve the implementation as the project develops.

04

Deliver with clarity

Prepare the finished solution for use, explain important functionality and provide clear documentation or handover guidance where required.
