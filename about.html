I'm Stanley Munyao, a Bachelor of Economics and Statistics student at the University of Nairobi. My academic interests include understanding how economies work, how data can explain patterns, and how evidence can support better decisions.

Alongside my studies, I'm developing practical skills in data analytics, programming, software and web development. I learn by working through concepts, building applications, analysing datasets and documenting what I discover.

This website brings together the projects I have worked on, the skills I am developing and the ideas I want to explore. I aim to be transparent about what I know, what I am learning and what I have built.
