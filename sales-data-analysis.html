Sales Data Analysis & Business Intelligence

An 800-order Kenyan retail dataset analysed in Excel to organise performance measures and surface useful trends.

Excel analysis complete

Workbook evidence below includes concept mockups built with sample data. The real project workbook captures are added as they are finalised.
Dataset

Dataset overview

The working dataset holds 800 retail orders in a Kenyan shilling transaction context, with product, category, payment and city detail on every row.

Objectives

Analytical objectives

  • Performance measuresSales revenue, order count, units and average order value.
  • Product viewHow products and categories compare.
  • Time patternsMonthly sales trends across the dataset.
  • SegmentsPayment method and city-level summaries.
Process

Data cleaning & preparation

The Excel work focuses on structuring the raw data first: consistent column types, removed duplicates and prepared analysis sheets before any summary was built.
